(A hallway. Late afternoon. A water fountain that does not work, a vending machine that does. TRADUCTORA is sitting on a wooden bench, a small black notebook open on her lap, drinking tea from a paper cup. I sits down at the other end of the bench.)

I: I hope it's not — I can move.

TRADUCTORA: It's fine. There's a bench.

I: ...

TRADUCTORA: You are not here for the hearings.

I: No.

TRADUCTORA: I can tell. People here for the hearings sit differently.

I: How.

TRADUCTORA: *Con más cuidado. Como si la silla también pudiera ser citada.*

---

I: Can I ask what you do.

TRADUCTORA: I translate.

I: Between —

TRADUCTORA: Between whoever does not share the language.

I: Today.

TRADUCTORA: Today there are eleven cases. I am between the lawyer and the family in nine of them. *Eso es lo que dice mi contrato.*

I: And the other two.

TRADUCTORA: The other two have witnesses from places where the language they speak is — not the official one. So for those I am between the lawyer, the family, and a different intermediary. *Una segunda traducción dentro de la primera.*

I: That sounds —

TRADUCTORA: Slow. Yes.

---

(TRADUCTORA writes briefly in the notebook, closes it. The cover is worn at the corner.)

I: I am writing about — a problem of communication that interests me.

TRADUCTORA: Which one.

I: When two people who want to understand each other cannot.

TRADUCTORA: *Hay varios canales por los que eso pasa.* You will not be able to write about it cleanly.

I: That is what I am finding.

TRADUCTORA: It is a job, finding which channel is the one that failed. I do not have time to do it well in the hearings.

I: Do you do it later.

TRADUCTORA: ...

TRADUCTORA: Some of it. *Algo escribo aquí.*

(She does not show the notebook.)

---

I: You said you translate between whoever does not share the language. Is that — all you do.

TRADUCTORA: *Eso es lo que dice el contrato.*

I: But.

TRADUCTORA: ...

TRADUCTORA: There are things the witness will say in the way they say them, and there are ways of rendering it that will be heard correctly and ways that will not. *No es lo mismo.* The words and the meaning do not always travel together. The lawyer needs one. The record needs the other. Someone has to choose.

I: Who chooses.

TRADUCTORA: Whoever is in the room and has the languages.

I: That is a strange position.

TRADUCTORA: *No es posición. Es lo que pasa.* If no one chose, there would be — nothing. Silence. The cases do not allow for silence. *Hay que llenar el aire con algo.* ^fill-the-air

I: And what you put in the air —

TRADUCTORA: *Es lo que pude.*

---

I: Are there things you have not been able to render at all.

TRADUCTORA: ...

TRADUCTORA: There is a word, in the language of one of the families today, that means *the silence after a question the questioner does not realize is an answer*. You cannot translate it into French or English or Spanish. You have to translate it as silence. Then the lawyer hears silence and asks the question again, and the witness — *que ya respondió* — does not know what to do. *Y la cara que pone no aparece en el acta.*

I: What do you do.

TRADUCTORA: I say the word in the original. Then the lawyer asks me what it means. Then I explain. Then it is no longer the same word.

I: Because.

TRADUCTORA: Because by the time it is in their language it has been carried through me. *Y la palabra se acuerda de quien la lleva.* ^word-remembers-carrier

I: ...

---

(She glances at her watch. The notebook is on the bench beside her. Through the open cover the I sees, briefly: a list of words, none in an alphabet the I recognizes, each followed by a short phrase in Spanish, none of the phrases the same length as the word. She closes it without looking.)

I: ¿Puedo preguntarte cómo te vas a llamar — en lo que estoy escribiendo?

TRADUCTORA: *No me des un nombre.*

I: Why.

TRADUCTORA: The intérpretes who appear in records are the intérpretes who failed. The ones who did the work are not named.

I: I did not know that.

TRADUCTORA: It is not a rule. *Es un patrón. Lo notarás si lo buscas.*

---

(She drinks the last of the tea, stands.)

TRADUCTORA: My next hearing is in seven minutes. The lawyer is — *él cree que el problema es la palabra.*

I: And it isn't.

TRADUCTORA: *El problema es que el canal entre lo que ella dice y lo que él puede oír no existe todavía.* And no one is going to build it in seven minutes.

I: What do you do then.

TRADUCTORA: I do what I can. *Elijo cuáles pérdidas tomar.* I cannot avoid them all. I decide which.

I: And the lawyer —

TRADUCTORA: The lawyer will think I translated. *Y en cierto modo lo hice.*

(She leaves. The notebook goes into her bag without opening again. I remains on the bench. The cup of tea she did not finish is still on the wood, faintly warm.)

---

(There is a question I did not ask in time. *Cuál pérdida tomaste hoy.* The hallway is empty. The next hearing began two minutes ago. The seven minutes were not for the question.)
