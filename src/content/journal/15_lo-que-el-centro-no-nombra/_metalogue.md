I and a friend, hablando. We have not seen each other in months. There is wine, or there is no wine; it doesn't matter. The conversation has gotten to politics, the way it does.

AMIGA: So what do you think.

I: About what specifically.

AMIGA: Everything. The election. The protests. The whole — you know what I mean.

I: I think the categories we'd use to talk about it are part of what we'd be talking about.

AMIGA: That's not an answer.

I: I know.

AMIGA: I'm not asking you for a take. I just want to know where you are.

I: That's what I'm trying to say. Where I am isn't a place on the line you're drawing.

AMIGA: There's a line?

I: When you ask me where I am, you're asking which side. That's a line. The question puts me on it before I answer.

AMIGA: Fine. *Are you on the line.*

I: I'm on the line in the sense that I have to live somewhere. I'm not on the line in the sense that any answer I give will be misread.

AMIGA: Misread by who.

I: By whoever is using the line.

AMIGA: I'm not *using* it. I'm asking you a question.

I: The question is using it.

---

She is quiet for a moment. She knows me. She has known me for a long time. We have been on the same side of many things. She is wondering whether something has changed.

AMIGA: When did you start talking like this.

I: Like what.

AMIGA: Like you're outside it.

I: I'm not outside it. That's what I keep trying to say. There is [[journal:smash-laterally-iii#^no-outside|no outside]]. The thing I'm describing isn't a position next to the line; it's a position about the line.

AMIGA: That's still outside.

I: It feels outside. From inside the line, anything that questions the line feels outside.

AMIGA: Or maybe you've drifted, and you're calling the drift sophistication.

---

She does not say this meanly. She says it the way you tell someone that their shirt is on inside out.

I: That's possible.

AMIGA: You think it's possible?

I: I think the position I am trying to articulate is structurally indistinguishable, from where you are, from drift. That doesn't mean it's the same thing as drift. But I can't show you it isn't, because the showing would require a vocabulary we don't share.

AMIGA: We share a vocabulary. We've shared one for fifteen years.

I: We share a vocabulary in which one of us is on the left.

AMIGA: One of us?

I: Both of us, until recently.

AMIGA: So that's what changed.

I: I haven't changed what I want. I've changed what I think the question is.

AMIGA: Same thing.

I: It isn't.

AMIGA: From where I'm sitting it is.

---

This is the bind. She is not wrong from where she sits. From where she sits the change in what I think the question is *is* a change in what I want, because what I want is only knowable through how I name it, and how I name it has changed. The naming and the wanting are not separable from her position. They might not be separable from any position. I am not sure they are separable from mine.

I: What do you want me to say.

AMIGA: I want you to say where you are.

I: If I say where I am, you will translate it back into the line. Whatever I say, you will hear which side it's closer to. The thing I want to say can't survive that translation.

AMIGA: Then maybe the thing you want to say isn't worth saying.

I: That's the most honest version of it, yes.

AMIGA: I don't mean it as an insult.

I: I know.

AMIGA: I just mean — at some point you have to *land*. You have to vote. You have to march. You have to be counted.

I: I do all of those things.

AMIGA: Then you're on the line.

I: I'm acting on the line. I'm not standing on it.

AMIGA: I don't understand the difference.

I: I'm not sure I do either. I just know they are not the same.

---

She leaves, or I leave, or we keep drinking and talk about something else. There is no resolution because what we were doing was not the kind of thing that resolves. She thinks I have drifted. I think she has not yet seen what I have started to see, or has seen it and decided seeing it doesn't help. She may be right about the second. The position I was trying to describe is one that cannot be defended without performing exactly the move it tries to refuse — and so I did not defend it. I just kept refusing to answer her question in her terms, which from inside her terms is the same as not answering it at all.

The chair organized the room before either of us got there. I have started to see the chair. The friendship is real. The disagreement is not a disagreement. The bind is the bind.
