I: I want to ask you about the lateral move.

GIÁP: That is not what you mean.

I: It is.

GIÁP: It is the surface of what you mean. Ask again.

I: I want to ask you about Điện Biên Phủ.

GIÁP: Closer. Ask once more.

I: ...I want to ask whether the cleverness can be kept without the cost.

GIÁP: There. That is the question.

TEACHER: I thought he might never get there.

GENERAL: They never do, the first time.

I: I'm going to try to keep three of you straight. Is that all right?

GIÁP: Try.

---

TEACHER: You understood what I did. You wrote it down well enough.

I: I think so.

TEACHER: Tell it back to me.

I: The French built a frame around the contest in which their advantages were structurally decisive. You refused the frame. You moved your artillery in a register the French had not classified as possible. The contest, as the French had named it, became irrelevant.

TEACHER: Yes. And the principle?

I: Don't smash the top. Operate on the frame the top is sitting in.

TEACHER: A little better. Try once more.

I: ...The top is held up by the frame. You don't have to remove the top. You only have to move the frame.

TEACHER: Yes. And once you have moved the frame, what does the top become?

I: ...A piece of furniture in a room nobody is in anymore.

TEACHER: Good.

I: That is the part that excited me when I started writing.

TEACHER: I know. That is why you came to me first.

---

GENERAL: Now ask the part you didn't write down.

I: What part.

GENERAL: How many it took.

I: To do what.

GENERAL: To move the frame.

I: I — wrote about that. The casualty estimates. The protracted war.

GENERAL: You wrote it as a paragraph. Tell it again as an arithmetic.

I: ...

GENERAL: Eight thousand of my men dead at Điện Biên Phủ alone. Fifteen thousand wounded. To take a valley. To prove that the artillery could be put where the French had decided it could not. Tell me what each of those bodies bought. ^generals-arithmetic

I: They bought the airfield falling.

GENERAL: How much was the airfield worth in bodies.

I: No sé cómo hacer este cálculo.

GENERAL: I do. I did it every night for thirty years.

I: ...

GENERAL: He told you the principle. The principle is real. But the principle does not move the artillery. The artillery is moved by men who are also tired and also hungry and most of whom do not understand the principle. They move it because I have told them to move it and because there is nothing else for them to do. Some are killed by snipers on the way up. Some are killed by their own equipment. Some die of disease in the assembly area. None of them will see the airfield fall, because they will be dead before it does.

I: I don't know what to do with what you're saying.

GENERAL: That is a different question than what you asked him.

---

GIÁP: You are upset.

I: Yes.

GIÁP: With which of us.

I: I — I don't know how to think about you when you separate like this.

GIÁP: That is the right thing to be upset about.

I: Why.

GIÁP: Because you came here to keep us separate. You wanted his lesson and you did not want his ledger. So you wrote me as three. So you could take what you wanted from each.

I: That's not — I —

GIÁP: It is not an accusation. It is a description of how your mind works on a man like me. Your mind needs us to be three because three is the only way you can hold us. I am telling you what you have done, not blaming you for it.

I: But you were one person.

GIÁP: I was. The cleverness and the cost were the same person doing the same thing. The man who taught the principle and the man who counted the bodies were one man. There is no version of me where they were separate. You write me as three because the alternative is to hold me as one, and that is harder for you than it was for me. ^one-person

I: ...

GIÁP: It was not easier for me. It was only mandatory.

---

I: Then what do I do with the lesson.

GIÁP: Whatever you wanted to do. But not without the ledger.

I: I don't have a ledger.

GIÁP: Then you do not have the lesson either. You have a slogan. The slogan will fail you at the moment you need the lesson. ^slogan-warning

I: When is that moment.

GIÁP: When the contest you are in is rigged against you and the only way through is to refuse the contest and pay for the refusal in something that matters to you.

I: ...

GIÁP: There have been others. Smaller scales. Single bodies. People who refused contests their countries were running and paid in years they could not get back. You will recognize them. They will not call themselves lateral movers. The ones who did the move and called it that are usually the ones who didn't pay.

I: How will I recognize the ones who paid.

GIÁP: They are the ones still paying.

I: That is not an answer.

GIÁP: I know. It is also the answer.

I: I don't have a way to find them yet.

GIÁP: I noticed.

I: Then who am I going to find next.

GIÁP: Whoever is also one when you meet him.
