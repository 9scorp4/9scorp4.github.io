The bar is empty except for the two of us. The promoter has lifted her glass twice without drinking, and now she sets it down. She is in her seventies. She has been in this business since before I was born. I have wanted to ask her something for a long time and now there is no one to overhear.

**I:** I want to ask you about a call.

**PROMOTER:** I take many calls.

**I:** Not a specific call. The structure of calls. Someone phones, there has been an incident at the previous venue, the artist has collapsed, the question is whether tonight's booking still stands. You take the call. What do you say.

**PROMOTER:** It depends.

**I:** On what.

**PROMOTER:** On whether the artist is coming.

**I:** That isn't really the answer.

**PROMOTER:** It is, though. If the artist is coming, the show happens. If the artist is not coming, the show does not happen. My job is not to decide whether the artist is coming. My job is to decide whether the room is ready.

**I:** But the artist has collapsed.

**PROMOTER:** The artist's people will tell me if the artist is not coming.

**I:** And if they don't tell you.

**PROMOTER:** Then the artist is coming.

**I:** And if the artist arrives in a state where another collapse is foreseeable.

**PROMOTER:** That is not a question my job permits me to ask.

**I:** Why.

**PROMOTER:** Because if it were a question my job permitted me to ask, my job would be something else.

*She drinks. A long pause.*

**I:** I want to be honest with you. I am trying to understand whether you could have refused.

**PROMOTER:** I could not.

**I:** Why not.

**PROMOTER:** Because the refusal was not in the role.

**I:** But you were a person. You had information. You could see what was in front of you.

**PROMOTER:** I was a person *occupying a role*. The information I had was the information the role permitted me to process. What you can see, in any seat, is partly determined by which seat. I am not making excuses. I am describing a structure.

**I:** And the structure was such that no one in your position could have refused.

**PROMOTER:** Many people occupied my position. Across many years and many cities. The position itself was not built to refuse. If you replaced me with someone more compassionate, more medically informed, more attentive to early warning signs, the position still would not have refused, because the position is what makes the show happen. The function of the position is non-refusal. Refusal would dissolve the function.

**I:** So the failure was structural.

**PROMOTER:** The failure was not a failure. The position performed its function correctly. The failure — if it is a failure — was that the function existed in that shape at all. That is a different conversation. It is not a conversation I can have from my seat.

*She turns her glass on the bar.*

**I:** Do you think about it.

**PROMOTER:** Claro que pienso en eso. Of course I think about it.

**I:** What do you think.

**PROMOTER:** I think the people who write about it want me to be guilty. I think being guilty would be a comfort, because guilt is locatable. If I am the one who killed him, then there is someone who killed him, and the rest of the world is innocent. I am not interested in providing that comfort. I did not kill him. The role I occupied did not kill him. The whole shape of the thing killed him. The whole shape of the thing included me. The whole shape of the thing included you, sitting on the other side of this bar, listening to records made by a man my role helped keep on stages he could not survive. You are not innocent either. We are sitting here together.

**I:** That isn't what I came to hear.

**PROMOTER:** I know.

**I:** I think I came expecting either you to refuse the role retrospectively or to defend it.

**PROMOTER:** I will do neither.

**I:** Why.

**PROMOTER:** Because both would be lies. To refuse the role retrospectively would be to pretend I had a choice I did not have. To defend the role would be to pretend the role was good. The honest thing is to describe it. I described it. There is nothing else.

*A long silence.*

**I:** There is another version of this conversation I could be having. With another figure. Not in this room. Not in this decade. A figure that occupies the same structural position you occupied, but in a different cultural channel. A position where refusal would have been corrective but is not in the role. I wonder if that figure would say what you have said.

**PROMOTER:** I don't know that figure.

**I:** No. Of course not.

**PROMOTER:** But I will tell you this. If the figure tells you what you want to hear, you should not believe the figure. The function of a position without refusal is non-refusal. If a position appears to refuse, examine the position. It is performing something. It is not refusing.

*She finishes her drink.*

**PROMOTER:** Whoever is keeping you on the stage you cannot survive, right now, is not the one you can ask whether you should be on the stage.

*She gets up. The encounter is over.*
