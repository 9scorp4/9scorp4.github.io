# ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# │ ON AI COLLABORATION
# └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This site is grown with the help of AI tools — Claude, specifically.
Not as ghost-author; as assistant. The difference matters.

The garden blocks training crawlers (see robots.txt).
Not because AI is bad, but because extraction without consent is.
Commercial models scraping creative work to sell it back cheaper
is not collaboration — it's a supply-chain inversion.

Collaboration, by contrast, requires presence:
  a prompt, a reply, a correction, a loop.
  The AI brings pattern; the human brings care.
  Neither is the other's substrate.

If you're building with AI, build *with* it — eyes open, hands on, in dialogue.
If you're training on others' work, ask first.

The soil is not your substrate.
The garden is not your feedstock.
But if you come to tend, there's a spare trowel by the gate.
